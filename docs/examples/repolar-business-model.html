1. Executive summary

Repolar Diagnostics develops RepoScore, software that estimates the risk of life-threatening arrhythmias in patients with inherited channelopathies such as long QT syndrome (LQTS) and short QT syndrome (SQTS), from 12-lead and wearable ECG recordings combined with genetic findings. A second product, RepoTox, screens drug candidates for QT prolongation and cardiotoxicity for pharmaceutical companies.

We want to open a Swiss research and validation site in 2027 and are evaluating Canton Bern, because of its cardiovascular research and its university hospital.

What we are looking for in Bern
  • Research groups in cardiac electrophysiology and genetic arrhythmia syndromes (LQTS, SQTS, arrhythmogenic cardiomyopathy).
  • Experimental models of arrhythmia, such as transgenic animal models, to validate our risk markers.
  • Expertise in cardiotoxicity testing with human iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes for RepoTox.
  • Clinical partners for a registry study on personalized risk stratification of channelopathy patients.
  • Know-how in pharmacology of anti-arrhythmic drugs and drug effects on cardiac repolarization.

2. Problem

  • Inherited arrhythmia syndromes affect about 1 in 2,000 people and are a leading cause of sudden cardiac death in the young.
  • Today's risk scores use the QT interval alone and miss many patients at risk, while others get defibrillators they never need.
  • About one in ten drug candidates fails in development because of cardiac safety findings, often late and at high cost.

3. Solution and technology

RepoScore

  • Deep-learning model on ECG morphology, heart-rate dynamics and genotype.
  • Works with 12-lead ECGs and with single-lead wearable recordings.
  • Retrospective validation on 3,400 patients: AUC 0.86 for arrhythmic events.

RepoTox

  • In-vitro assay on human iPSC-derived cardiomyocytes with field-potential recordings.
  • Combined with in-silico action-potential models of repolarization.
  • Service for pharma: report within 3 weeks per compound.
