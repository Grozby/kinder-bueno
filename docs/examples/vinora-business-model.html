1. Executive summary

Vinora AgTech helps wine growers and fruit farmers use less water and fewer fungicides. Drones take multispectral images of the fields, wireless sensors measure soil moisture, and our models predict fungal disease and yield per parcel. Farmers get a weekly spraying and irrigation plan in a mobile app.

We want to open a Swiss subsidiary in 2027 to serve vineyards in western Switzerland and are evaluating Canton Bern, including the Lake Biel wine region.

What we are looking for in Bern
  • Research partners in plant pathology, especially downy and powdery mildew in grapevines.
  • Expertise in remote sensing and multispectral image analysis of crops.
  • Soil science and irrigation research for climate adaptation in agriculture.
  • Test fields and pilot farms for precision viticulture.

2. Problem

  • Vineyards are among the most heavily sprayed crops in Europe; growers spray on fixed schedules.
  • Hotter, drier summers make irrigation decisions harder, and water is becoming scarce.

3. Solution

  • Drone flights every 10 days with a multispectral camera; vegetation indices per vine row.
  • Low-power soil moisture sensors (LoRaWAN), one per hectare.
  • Disease risk model from weather data and canopy images; yield forecast at veraison.
