What Bern offers a company, with every fact sourced.

A business model goes in. The labs, experts and projects that fit come out, each linked to its public page.

ARTORG · Diabetes Center Berne · UniBE labs — Apertus, bge-m3, Postgres, Docker

Bern Fit helps the agency answer one question fast: a company is thinking of moving to Canton Bern; what does Bern’s research ecosystem offer it? Every answer comes from public pages and links back to them.

The problem

A company sends its business model. The agency has to show what Bern offers it.

Hundreds of pages

The knowledge sits on faculty, institute, lab and centre pages, each written its own way.

Other words

The company says “glucose sensors”; the lab page says “continuous glucose monitoring”.

Bern Fit · 2

Today this is manual: someone who knows the ecosystem reads pages and writes an email. It is slow, and it depends on who you ask. Keyword search misses the match because companies and labs describe the same thing in different words.

Bern Fit · 3

The analysis takes about 30 seconds. A reference counts in the score only when one of its verified topic facts matches a need. The pitch is written only from the references the team selected.

The guarantee

No invented facts. Every fact is a literal slice of a public page.

100% traceable

Cited sentences

Each “why” and pitch sentence cites claim ids like [11258]. Uncited ones are removed.

Bern Fit · 4 · eval/report.md

Apertus must return a quote for every fact. We match the quote against the stored page text and save the exact page text, or we drop the fact. That check is what makes a small open model safe to use here.

Bern Fit · 5 · eval/case_report.md, 4 example business models

We wrote four fictional business models: Glucora (glucose sensors), Repolar (cardiac arrhythmia and cardiotoxicity testing), Precisa (surgical navigation and robotics) and Vinora (vineyard agritech). The first three should fit Bern, the last one should not. The board gets all four right; Vinora’s 12 is one weak match, a climate scientist for a climate-adaptation need. The score shows its gaps too: for Glucora, “no matching infrastructure in our sources”.

Bern Fit · 6 · eval/report.md, 18 gold queries over 340 pages

Glucose is weakest because some of its experts are linked only to hospital and project pages, not to a lab, so no ranking change brings them in. Personal expertise comes from profile pages; people without one show their lab’s topics, labelled as such, instead of pretending. The judge is the same model family as the extractor, so we treat it as a signal, not a measurement.

Sources you can steer

Ranked pages

All 340 pages scored by verified facts, judge rate, quote survival and 👍/👎.

Block or prioritise

A blocked page’s facts leave search and cases at once. Undo is one click.

Add a website

From the UI, or one block in config/sources.yaml. Same pipeline, in the background.
